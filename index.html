Hazardous Employment

A darkly comedic fantasy guild-management roguelite where you recruit, equip, and arrange adventurers—but never control them in battle.

The heroes fight monsters. You fight payroll.

  • Guild management
  • Auto battler
  • Roguelite
  • Single-player
How the guild works ↓
Administrative judgment required

Prepare the expedition. Live with the paperwork.

Your decisions determine who goes, where they stand, what they carry, and whether the company can afford the consequences.

01

Assemble a qualified* team

Recruit adventurers with different classes, potential, and wage expectations. Balance talent against a payroll that remains distressingly real.

02

Accept reasonable contracts

Study the danger, rewards, and staffing requirements. Arrange your formation and decide how much risk the guild can absorb.

03

Observe the consequences

Expeditions play out automatically. Survivors gain experience and injuries; casualties create vacancies, obligations, and occasionally useful heirlooms.

*Qualifications are not independently verified.
